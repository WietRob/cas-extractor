## Analysis

### Why No R5 Reduction?

httpie codebase analysis:
- **Few constructor chains**: `ClassName().method()` pattern rarely used
- **Main patterns**: `self.method()`, `super().__init__()`, `obj.method()` (variable)
- **Variable chains**: `f.read().decode()` — H1/H2 territory
